# Knowledge Page Reviewer V1

## Purpose

This document is the approval checklist for Linear Algebra Bridge knowledge pages.

A page is approved only when product positioning, bilingual content, mathematics, visuals, interaction, accessibility, and responsive layout all pass.

## 1. Scope and Product Fit

- Is the page listed under the correct approved chapter and section?
- Does it serve pre-class language and concept readiness?
- Are English-Chinese terminology, pronunciation, and classroom expressions visibly the primary experience?
- Does it avoid becoming a full textbook chapter or problem set?
- Is the intended page family identified?
- Are prerequisite ideas stated without unnecessary reteaching?
- Does every major module serve a clear learning purpose?

## 2. Bilingual Content Review

- Is the English title standard and natural?
- Is the Chinese title the accepted academic term?
- Are translations mathematical rather than literal?
- Are Academic English, professional Chinese, and intuition clearly separated?
- Are important word forms or confusion pairs handled?
- Do classroom sentences sound like plausible lecture language?
- Does each audio control name and play the correct term or sentence?
- Is pronunciation presented consistently?
- Does Chinese support remain visible without overwhelming the English target language?

## 3. Mathematical Accuracy Review

### Definitions and Conditions

- Are all required conditions stated?
- Are zero/nonzero conditions correct?
- Are assumptions about fields, dimensions, bases, or inner products explicit when relevant?
- Are examples and non-examples valid?
- Are theorem statements distinguished from intuition?

### Matrices and Operations

- Do dimensions match for addition, multiplication, and composition?
- Is multiplication order correct?
- Are row and column conventions consistent?
- Are elementary row operations legal and correctly applied?
- Are matrix equality, equivalence, similarity, and congruence distinguished?
- Is an inverse treated as a matrix inverse rather than entrywise reciprocal?

### Vectors and Spaces

- Are vectors distinguished from their coordinate columns?
- Is a subspace shown to contain zero and satisfy closure conditions when relevant?
- Are span, linear combination, independence, basis, rank, and dimension used distinctly?
- Does a claimed basis actually span and remain independent?
- Are kernel, image, codomain, row space, and column space distinguished?

### Eigenvalues and Geometry

- Is every eigenvector nonzero and does it satisfy `Av=λv`?
- Are algebraic and geometric multiplicities distinguished when relevant?
- Are similarity and diagonalization conditions correct?
- Does an orthogonality claim use the stated inner product?
- Does determinant scaling match magnitude and orientation?
- Are quadratic-form and definiteness claims supported by correct conditions?

## 4. Notation and Typography Review

- Is notation consistent across overview, visual, example, and Quick Check?
- Are matrices aligned and fully bracketed?
- Are vectors styled consistently?
- Are superscripts, subscripts, norms, transposes, inverses, determinants, and inner products clear?
- Are dimensions visible when compatibility is being taught?
- Do line breaks preserve equality and logical order?
- Are exact and approximate values clearly distinguished?

## 5. Hero Card Review

- Is the central mathematical object immediately identifiable?
- Does the card communicate the page's core relationship?
- Does every arrow, colour, region, and label have a defined meaning?
- Is the card topic-specific rather than copied from an unrelated page?
- Does it remain correct as a static image?
- Is it readable on desktop and mobile?
- Are matrix brackets, coordinates, and labels intact?
- At 100% browser zoom, do the before matrix, operation, arrow, and after matrix remain separated without overlap?

## 6. Visualization and Interaction Review

- Does the visual answer a named mathematical question?
- Are all objects, coordinates, and transformations correct?
- Does motion follow the displayed matrix or operation?
- Is one mathematical action introduced per step?
- Does each step state what changed and what stayed invariant?
- Do highlights synchronize corresponding representations?
- Can essential content be understood without dragging or animation?
- Are controls labelled, keyboard usable, and compatible with reduced motion?
- Does the complete interactive card boundary remain visible, including around light-coloured result panels?

## 7. Learning Experience Review

- Can a student identify what to listen for in class?
- Can a student connect the main spoken terms to their written English and Chinese meanings?
- Is mathematical depth limited to what the language needs?
- Does “Why it matters” state a genuine mathematical need?
- Is the first example small enough for pre-class use?
- Is there normally only one simple example?
- Does the page avoid unnecessary repetition?
- Is the page length proportional to the topic?
- Does the summary claim readiness rather than mastery?
- Are external resources specific and accurately described?

## 8. Worked Example Review

- Does the example use the target concept rather than a hidden advanced method?
- Is one action performed per step?
- Are English action phrases paired with the mathematical step?
- Are intermediate matrices and equations correct?
- Is the final result interpreted, not merely displayed?
- Does any reveal interaction preserve access to earlier steps?
- Does each animated step pair the English instruction, Chinese meaning, symbolic operation, and visible change?
- Does the sequence stop once the target vocabulary and action are clear?

## 9. Classroom English Review

- Are there four to seven high-value sentences?
- Do they cover definition, operation, condition, or interpretation?
- Is the highlighted phrase the part students need to recognize?
- Is the Chinese meaning natural?
- Are listening cues selective and accurate?
- Does audio match visible punctuation and terminology closely enough to follow?

## 10. Quick Check Review

- Does the Quick Check test understanding rather than visual recognition?
- Are at least two types of understanding tested?
- Is there explanatory feedback for correct and incorrect choices?
- Are distractors plausible but mathematically unambiguous?
- Is a classroom-language item included where useful?
- Does at least half of the check directly assess terminology, pronunciation/listening, classroom instructions, or notation reading?
- Are lengthy calculations excluded from the standard preview check?
- Does completion state work with keyboard and touch?

## 11. Responsive and Accessibility Review

- Has the page been inspected at 1440, 1024, 768, 390, and 320 px widths?
- Does desktop sidebar and mobile drawer navigation work?
- Is there no unintended horizontal page overflow?
- Do matrices, formulas, labels, and diagrams remain readable?
- Is Chinese support sufficiently large and high contrast?
- Are focus states visible?
- Do controls have descriptive accessible names?
- Is colour accompanied by another cue?
- Is meaningful alternative text available for diagrams?

## 12. Consistency Review

- Does the page preserve the navy/brick-red/gold academic brand?
- Does it use the established bilingual hierarchy and typography?
- Are section labels and card styles consistent with the product family?
- Are deviations justified by the topic, accessibility, or usability?
- Does the page follow the Course Map, Chapter Index, page template, and visual guidelines?

## 13. Self-Improvement Loop

If any item fails:

1. record the exact issue;
2. classify it as scope, language, mathematics, visual, interaction, responsive, or accessibility;
3. correct the source content or implementation;
4. repeat the relevant review, not only the visible symptom;
5. run the full completion gate again after a mathematical or structural change.

## 14. Approval Record

Record:

- Page status: Draft / In Review / Approved
- Content review: Pending / Passed
- Mathematical review: Pending / Passed
- Visual review: Pending / Passed
- Responsive review: Pending / Passed
- Accessibility review: Pending / Passed
- Human review: Pending / Approved
- Reviewer notes:
- Date:

No page may be marked Approved while any required review remains Pending or Failed.
