## Priority and Dependency Notes

- Permutations and inversions support the formal definition of the determinant.
- Determinant properties support both triangularization and cofactor methods.
- Minors and cofactors precede cofactor expansion and connect back to adjugate matrices.
- Cramer's Rule requires a square coefficient matrix with nonzero determinant.
- Geometric meaning is an application and interpretation, not the definition of a determinant.
