## Priority and Dependency Notes

- Mappings and bijections are emphasized in the syllabus and should precede later uses of isomorphism.
- Matrix language is introduced here before the fuller treatment of matrix operations in Chapter 2.
- Elementary row operations, Gaussian elimination, and solution classification form the core Chapter 1 procedure sequence.
- Chapter 2 may assume familiarity with basic matrix types and row operations, but should not assume the complete matrix theory developed later.
