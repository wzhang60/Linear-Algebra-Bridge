## Priority and Dependency Notes

- Eigenvalue and eigenvector definitions precede multiplicity and diagonalization.
- Similarity and diagonalization depend on change-of-basis concepts from Chapter 5.
- Algebraic and geometric multiplicities support the diagonalizability criterion.
- Real symmetric matrices and orthogonal diagonalization connect forward to Chapter 7.
- Linear-transformation matrices require explicit bases; the mapping and its matrix representation must remain distinct.
