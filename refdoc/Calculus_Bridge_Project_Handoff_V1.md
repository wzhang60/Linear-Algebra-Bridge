\# Calculus Bridge Project Handoff V1





\## 1. Project Overview





Project Name:



Calculus Bridge





Purpose:



Create a bilingual (English + Chinese) calculus preview platform for university students.



The goal is NOT to replace a calculus textbook.



The goal is:



\- Help students preview concepts before class.

\- Learn English mathematical terminology.

\- Understand key concepts in Chinese.

\- Become familiar with classroom language.







\## 2. Current Product Positioning

\# 7. Important Design Principles





\## Hero Card





Hero Card should:



\- Explain the concept first.

\- Avoid arbitrary examples.

\- Avoid unexplained formulas.

\- Prefer intuitive relationships before formal definitions.







\## Visual Understanding





Visualizations must:



\- Represent mathematical meaning.

\- Avoid decorative animation.

\- Maintain readable labels.

\- Avoid overlapping objects and formulas.







\## Mathematical Formatting





Important formulas:



\- Must be fully visible.

\- Must not be clipped.

\- Avoid ambiguous slash notation.

\- Use proper fraction formatting.







\## Navigation





Navigation hierarchy:





Chapter



↓



Textbook Section



↓



Knowledge Topic





All levels should be clickable.





Active item:



\- Highlighted

\- Darker

\- Clearly selected
