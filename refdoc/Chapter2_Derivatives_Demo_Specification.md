\# Knowledge Point 1



\# Derivative Concept



\## Purpose



Introduce the fundamental idea of derivative.



Students should understand:



A derivative describes how a quantity changes at a specific moment.





\## Key Concepts



English:



Derivative



Chinese:



导数





Related concepts:



\- Rate of Change

\- Instantaneous Rate of Change

\- Tangent Line

\- Slope





\## Visual Requirement



Recommended:



Show how a secant line approaches a tangent line.



Explain:



Average change rate



↓



Instantaneous change rate



↓



Derivative





\## Classroom English



Examples:



"Find the derivative of the function."



"The derivative represents the rate of change."



"The slope of the tangent line gives the derivative."

\# Knowledge Point 2



\# Quotient Rule



\## Purpose



Teach how to differentiate a function divided by another function.





\## Key Concepts



English:



Quotient Rule



Chinese:



商法则





Formula:



\\\[

\\left(\\frac{f}{g}\\right)'=

\\frac{f'g-fg'}{g^2}

\\]





\## Why Do We Need It?



Explain:



Why a simple differentiation rule cannot directly solve division of functions.





Example:



\\\[

y=\\frac{x^2}{sinx}

\\]





\## Visual Requirement



Explain:



The numerator and denominator both change.



The final change depends on both.





\## Step-by-Step Example



Structure:



1\. Identify numerator and denominator.

2\. Apply Quotient Rule.

3\. Calculate derivatives.

4\. Simplify answer.

\# Knowledge Point 3



\# Chain Rule



\## Purpose



Teach differentiation of composite functions.





\## Key Concepts



English:



Chain Rule



Chinese:



链式法则





Related vocabulary:



\- Inner Function

\- Outer Function

\- Composite Function





\## Why Do We Need It?



Explain:



Some functions contain another function inside.



Example:



\\\[

y=(x^2+1)^5

\\]





Students need to understand:



Outer function



and



Inner function.





\## Visual Requirement



Create visualization:



A change passes through multiple layers.



Example:



x



↓



inner function



↓



outer function



↓



final output





Explain:



The total change is the product of changes at each layer.





\## Step-by-Step Example



Structure:



1\. Identify inner and outer functions.

2\. Differentiate outer function.

3\. Differentiate inner function.

4\. Multiply the derivatives.
