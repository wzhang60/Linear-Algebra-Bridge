## Mathematical review highlights

- Determinant pages distinguish permutations, determinant properties, cofactors, Cramer conditions, and signed geometric scaling.
- Vector pages distinguish span, equivalent vector sets, independence, rank, subspaces, row/column spaces, solution spaces, image, and kernel.
- Abstract vector-space pages distinguish vectors from coordinate columns and keep basis direction explicit in transition matrices.
- Eigenvalue pages exclude the zero eigenvector, distinguish algebraic and geometric multiplicity, and state diagonalizability conditions.
- Euclidean-space pages name the inner product, distinguish orthogonal from orthonormal, and preserve the conditions for Gram–Schmidt, QR, and orthogonal transformations.
- Quadratic-form pages distinguish similarity from congruence, standard form from canonical form, and state inertia and positive-definiteness conditions.
- The Chapter 7 least-squares page uses the same residual and normal-equation convention as the Chapter 4 introduction.
