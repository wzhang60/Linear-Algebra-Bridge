## Revision after human review

Human review identified that the first batch of eleven pages had collapsed the static example and visual-recognition content, reducing the reference structure and omitting the controlled four-step action demonstration. On revision, every generated page restored the complete reference structure. Static examples are no longer presented as substitutes for action demonstrations.

A later human review found that imposing four ordered screens on every Concept and Structure topic made some Section 04 interactions artificial. The adaptive revision keeps stepwise players only for genuinely ordered procedures. The remaining topics now use directly selectable Compare and Classify, Synchronized Views, Structure Map, Side-by-side Comparison, or Decision Board interactions. Identify / Inspect or Act / Observe / Connect remain learning functions, but they are no longer mandatory screen counts.
